## Questions, section by section

### 1. On Deck (front page)

Current rules: due within a day; requests with a deadline appear the day before; requests with no deadline appear after 4 days without a reply; circle-back notes appear when their time comes; big grants appear 10 days out; bills appear 3 days before the due date.

1. Is "the day before" right for requests with a deadline, or do some kinds need more lead time (for example anything that needs a document from someone else)?
2. What makes a grant or deadline "big"? Proposed: $25,000 and up, or anything Kevin marks priority. Should some types always count as big (fellowships, museum shows)?
3. Should calendar events show On Deck at all, or only when they need preparation?
4. Each area card shows the top 3 items. Right number?
5. Three scans a day (about 6:40am, 12:40pm and 5:40pm Eastern). Right times?
6. When something is snoozed, are tomorrow, 3 days and next week the right choices?

### 2. QAM page

1. Timeline: any milestones missing? Do Amanda Milan (New York), the LLC signature and Ogunquit have dates or target months yet?
2. Leads: should Sam's and Maureen's leads show too, or only Sean's? Should the team (Sam, Maureen, Tasneem, Kevin, Amy) be hidden, as it is now?
3. A lead counts as "quiet" after 21 days without contact, and then the page suggests going back through whoever introduced them. Is 21 days right?
4. Renewals: which gifts should be tracked, and when did each come in? (Couch Family Foundation is the example from the call.)
5. Applications: the tab reads the Airtable Funding table. Kevin's tracker stays read only. Should Claude copy Kevin's opportunities into the Funding table so there is one list, or keep them separate?

### 3. QAM people wiki (Airtable Contacts)

1. How far back should the backfill go for email and Granola: all time, or since a date?

5. CV approvals: one tap updates the CV, the CV PDF, the website and the Art Practice Master. Is the website repository for seanfader.com on GitHub, and what is it called?
